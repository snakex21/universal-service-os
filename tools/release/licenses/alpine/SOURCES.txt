USOS micro-Linux: Alpine Linux v3.24 packages (x86_64)

initramfs-usos (EFI/USOS/micro-linux) and initramfs-xp (XP package) contain
unmodified binaries from the Alpine packages below, pinned by SHA-256 in
tools/micro_linux.lock.json (packages) and taken from the Alpine 3.24.1
netboot initramfs-lts / modloop-lts (base userland and kernel modules).
USOS adds its own scripts and programs (usr/lib/usos, usos-init, usos-fb-ui).

Corresponding source: the Alpine aports tree at the commit recorded in each
package (.PKGINFO "commit"), which names the exact upstream tarball and the
Alpine patches, and the Alpine distfiles mirror:
  https://gitlab.alpinelinux.org/alpine/aports/-/tree/<commit>/<repo>/<origin>
  https://distfiles.alpinelinux.org/distfiles/v3.24/<tarball>
The USOS maintainer also offers the complete corresponding source of the
GPL/LGPL packages on request (SOURCE-OFFER.txt in the release).
